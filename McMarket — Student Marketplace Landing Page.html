How we launch

Starting on McTavish,
growing from there

We're launching with a real event before an app — a flea market on McTavish where students can exchange items directly and get early access to the platform.

In-person flea market on McTavish

Collecting items from classmates, encouraging donations, and running a bring-one, take-one exchange to seed the community.

Platform goes live for early users

Waitlist members get first access. University email verification active from day one.

Seasonal campaigns on social media

Highlighting relevant categories as the academic year moves — driving organic engagement around real student moments.

Expand to universities across Quebec

Concordia, Université de Montréal, Laval, and beyond — connecting students through a shared secondhand network.

Operating as a nonprofit (initially)

No transaction fees, no middleman. We facilitate exchanges between students without processing payments — keeping costs and complexity low.

Low startup costs

A flea market location on campus and a domain registration. We're not raising a seed round — we're starting with a table and a sign.

Long-term sustainability

As the platform grows, partnerships with local sustainable brands and student-oriented businesses support operations without compromising the mission.
